
MEDIRAG HEALTHCARE INFORMATION GUIDE

Purpose:
This document provides general educational health information.
It is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.

FEVER:
Fever can occur for many reasons, including infections.
Rest and drink fluids if you can do so safely.
Seek medical advice if fever persists, worsens, or causes concern.
Seek urgent medical care for confusion, difficulty breathing, seizures, or other severe symptoms.

HEADACHE:
Headaches can have many causes, including dehydration, stress, and illness.
Rest in a quiet environment and drink water if appropriate.
Consult a healthcare professional if the headache persists or repeatedly returns.
A sudden, extremely severe headache, weakness, confusion, or difficulty speaking requires urgent medical attention.

COUGH:
Cough can occur with respiratory infections and other conditions.
Cover your mouth when coughing and wash your hands regularly.
Seek medical advice if the cough persists or worsens.
Difficulty breathing or blue or grey lips require emergency medical care.

WHEN TO SEEK HELP:
This document cannot determine the cause or seriousness of an individual's symptoms.
Do not delay professional care when symptoms are severe or worsening.



DEHYDRATION:
Dehydration happens when the body loses more fluid than it takes in.
Possible signs include thirst, dark urine, and passing urine less often.
Drink fluids if you can safely do so.
Seek medical advice if symptoms persist or worsen.
Confusion, fainting, or severe weakness requires urgent medical assessment.

DIARRHEA:
Diarrhea means passing loose or watery stools more often than usual.
Oral rehydration solution (ORS), prepared according to the packet instructions, can help replace lost fluids and salts.
Continue appropriate feeding and seek advice from a healthcare professional.
Blood in the stool, severe dehydration, or inability to drink requires prompt medical attention.

MATERNAL HEALTH:
Pregnant people should attend recommended antenatal checkups with a qualified healthcare professional.
Seek urgent maternity care for heavy bleeding, severe abdominal pain, severe headache with vision changes, or difficulty breathing.
Do not delay care when serious symptoms occur.

CHILD HEALTH:
Children need age-appropriate vaccination and routine growth monitoring.
Caregivers should consult a qualified healthcare worker when a child is unwell.
Difficulty breathing, unusual difficulty waking, seizures, or inability to drink requires urgent medical assessment.

HIGH BLOOD PRESSURE:
High blood pressure may not cause noticeable symptoms.
A blood pressure measurement by a trained healthcare worker is needed to check it.
Attend recommended health checkups and follow the treatment plan prescribed by a healthcare professional.
Chest pain, severe difficulty breathing, or sudden weakness requires emergency medical attention.

HEALTH WORKER FOLLOW-UP:
A community health worker can help people find appropriate local health services and attend recommended follow-up visits.
Urgent symptoms must be referred promptly to appropriate medical services.
Protect personal health information and share it only with authorized people who need it for care.
